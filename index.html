How it works

1
Report

Tell us what you lost, when you lost it and the general location.

2
Review

A moderator reviews the report before it becomes public.

3
Match

Someone who finds a possible match can contact the team privately.

4
Verify & Return

Ownership is checked before the item is returned.

Safety First

Get Help

What LostFound Can Help With

Suitable lost property such as bags, clothing, keys and personal electronics during the proposed Darwin pilot.

What We Don't Handle

Missing people or animals, suspected criminal matters, dangerous items, private investigations or confrontations.

Items Requiring Referral

Items already held by police, transport operators or businesses should normally remain with those organisations. Cash, identification documents and high-value property may require special procedures.
