Safety First

Get Help

What We Can Help With

Lost bags, clothing, keys and suitable personal electronic devices during the Darwin pilot.

What We Don't Handle

Missing people, missing animals, suspected criminal matters, dangerous items or private investigations.

Safety & Referral

Dangerous items should not be handed to LostFound. Valuable property, identification documents and cash may require referral to an appropriate organisation.
